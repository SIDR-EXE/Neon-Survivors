## 🌌 What Is This?

**Neon Survivors** is a retro pixel-art **horde-survival roguelite**. You're a lone caped warrior dropped into an endless dark void. Your weapons fire **on their own**. Your only job is to **move, dodge, and choose your upgrades** while wave after wave of monsters closes in from every side.

Every kill drops XP. Every level-up deals three cards. Build a storm of fire, lightning and holy light — or get swallowed by the swarm.

**How long can you survive?**

### 💎 Progression

- **4 XP gem tiers** — 🟢 Small · 🔵 Medium · 🟡 Large · 🔴 Huge (boss, 25 XP)
- **3-card level-ups** — new weapons, **+25% damage**, **+20% attack speed**, or stat boosts
- **Stat cards** — 👢 Swift Boots · 💖 Vitality · 💚 Recovery · 🧲 Magnet
- **10% critical hits** for double damage, shown as big gold numbers

### 📈 Endless Difficulty

- Enemies spawn **faster and in bigger groups** every minute
- New enemies get **+15% HP** and **+3% speed** for every minute survived
- Bosses arrive **every 60 seconds**, more of them each time

### 🎨 Look, Feel & Sound

- 🎨 **PICO-8 inspired** 16-color pixel art, every sprite drawn in code
- 📺 **CRT scanlines** + vignette for a retro-monitor look
- 📳 Screen shake, hit-stop, particle bursts, floating damage numbers
- 🔊 **8-bit sound effects synthesized live** — zero audio files
- 🔠 Classic **Press Start 2P** arcade font
